OS Lab

A real x86 PC emulated in WebAssembly by v86. The guest has no network, and nothing you type leaves this tab.

Click the screen to type. Tab goes to the guest; Shift+Tab leaves the screen. Try uname -a or ls /bin on Linux, or tetris on FreeDOS.

Measurements

Emulator: v86 0.5.473 (BSD-2-Clause), loaded from jsDelivr. BIOS: SeaBIOS and VGABIOS (LGPL-3.0). Guest images: v86's copy/images (Buildroot Linux, FreeDOS; GPL-2.0). Everything is downloaded on demand from pinned versions and checked against SHA-256 hashes. Saves stay in this browser's IndexedDB.
